      Export the NAP master

      One row per NAP box: the master columns you can edit and import back, plus the computed status (read-only). Deactivated NAPs are listed with status inactive and active NO; this export is the only place they appear.

      Import: add or update NAP boxes

      Team GPS wins over imported coordinates. A NAP whose location came from a team's GPS during an audit keeps it; imported lat/lng are applied only to NAPs with Converge (seed) or manually entered coordinates, or none. Blank cells never erase data. Port statuses, audits, photos and dispatch history are never changed. active = NO deactivates a NAP (hidden from every list, map and dispatch; its open dispatches are cancelled); YES brings it back.

      Use the NAP Master import template, or a file exported above. Up to 5,000 rows per file. Nothing is saved until you press Apply.
